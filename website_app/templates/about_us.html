Our Story

VFF Group · Velvet Wash

Velvet Wash is the laundry and dry-cleaning division of VFF Group — bringing world-class garment care to Belagavi with free collection, transparent pricing, and operations you can trust. From everyday wash & fold to premium dry cleaning and commercial laundry, we serve homes, hotels, and businesses across Tilakwadi and Auto Nagar.

  • Professional & reliable service
  • Eco-friendly cleaning products
  • 100% customer satisfaction focus
  • VFF Group mobile app for booking & tracking

Leadership

Mrs. Deepa R Pattar

Founder & Chief Executive Officer — VFF Group

Mrs. Deepa R Pattar founded Velvet Wash Laundry & Dry Cleaning under VFF Group with a vision to raise laundry standards in Karnataka and beyond. Recognized as Women Entrepreneur of the Year 2022, she leads a team that combines textile-industry expertise with modern equipment, hygiene protocols, and customer-first service.

Under her leadership, Velvet Wash operates two Belagavi locations (Tilakwadi & Auto Nagar), serves thousands of customers through the VFF Group app, and is expanding nationally through a structured franchise program for entrepreneurs who want a proven laundry brand — not a startup from scratch.

  • Founder of Velvet Wash Laundry & Dry Cleaning
  • Women Entrepreneur of the Year 2022
  • Free collection & delivery across Belagavi
  • Franchise & partnership enquiries: franchise@vff-group.com
